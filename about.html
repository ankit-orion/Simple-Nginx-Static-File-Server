Why this exists

A small place for
big “what ifs.”

Localhost is a hands-on learning project. It brings together a Node.js server, a few HTML pages, and the styles that give them character.

The idea is simple: build each piece, understand how it works, then connect it to the next. From the first response to a complete page, every little improvement is something worth exploring.
